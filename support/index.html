Common questions

The app can't find my ring
  • Make sure Bluetooth is on and the ring is charged and close to your phone.
  • Allow the Nearby devices permission when asked. On Android 11 and earlier, also allow Location, which Android requires for Bluetooth scanning.
  • If the ring is paired with another phone or app, disconnect it there first.
My data stopped updating

Open the app to let it reconnect. Some phones stop background apps to save battery. If syncing keeps pausing, turn off battery optimisation for UbiFit in your phone's settings.

Where is my data stored?

The app stores readings, your profile, and ring names locally and has no UbiFit account or enabled cloud-sync service. Device backups and photos saved to your library may be managed or synced by your operating system and account settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I pair another ring?

Open the Ring tab and choose Pair a new ring. Your current ring stays remembered until the new connection succeeds and its pairing is saved. If you cancel or pairing fails, the previous pairing record is kept, although the Bluetooth connection may need to reconnect. Saved readings and ring names are kept when you switch. Names are matched by the ring's MAC address when available, with its platform identifier as a fallback.

How do I delete my data?

Clear the app's storage (Android: Settings → Apps → UbiFit → Storage → Clear storage) or delete the app to remove local app data. Replacing a ring does not delete that data or remove its OS Bluetooth pairing. Photos already saved to your library, device backups, and data on the ring must be managed separately.

Where do Ring camera photos go?

On supported iOS rings, Ring camera takes photos inside UbiFit. A photo stays in memory until you choose Save photo and allow the app to add it to your photo library. Saved photos may sync through your photo service according to your settings. This feature does not control the stock Camera app while UbiFit is in the background.

Are the readings medical-grade?

No. UbiFit is a wellness app and its readings are estimates. Please see a health professional about any health concern.
