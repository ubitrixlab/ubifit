Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026 · Ubitrix Intl Inc

The short version: UbiFit reads data from your smart ring over Bluetooth and keeps it on your phone. There is no UbiFit account. We do not receive, sell, or share your health data, and the app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking.

This policy explains what the UbiFit mobile app ("UbiFit", "the app") handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have. UbiFit is published by Ubitrix Intl Inc ("we", "us").

Information the app handles

All of the following is created on, and stays on, your device.

Ring health and activity data Heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen (SpO₂), skin temperature, stress, steps, activity, distance, calories, sleep stages, workouts, meditation sessions, and estimated blood pressure where your ring supports it.
Profile you enter Display name and, only if you choose to add them, date of birth, sex, height, weight, daily goals, a profile photo, and your preferred units.
Ring connection details Your ring's name, Bluetooth address, battery level, and firmware version, so the app can reconnect to it.

How the information is used

  • To show your measurements, trends, sleep, and progress toward your goals.
  • To personalise estimates. If you provide height, weight, age, or sex, the app writes them to your ring over Bluetooth so the ring can calculate distance and calories. They go to your ring and nowhere else.
  • To find, connect to, and stay connected with your ring.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any automated decision-making.

Where it is stored and for how long

  • Data is stored in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read.
  • Measurement history is kept for 90 days and then deleted automatically.
  • Your profile is kept until you change it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.
  • On Android, the app opts out of device backup, so its data is not copied to your Google account.

Sharing

The current version of UbiFit does not send your personal or health data off your device. We do not sell, rent, or share it with anyone, and the app does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting software.

If a future version offers optional features that need an internet connection, such as cloud backup or syncing across devices, we will update this policy first and ask for your permission in the app before any data leaves your phone.

Permissions

Bluetooth (nearby devices)To find, pair with, and read data from your ring.
LocationRequested only on Android 11 and earlier, where Android requires it for Bluetooth scanning. UbiFit does not read, store, or use your location.
Foreground serviceKeeps the connection to your ring alive while it syncs, shown as an ongoing notification.
PhotosUsed only when you pick a profile photo. The selected picture is resized and stored inside the app.

You can withdraw any permission in your phone's settings. Some features stop working without it.

Your choices

  • Edit or remove your profile details in the app at any time.
  • Use Forget this ring in the app to remove a paired ring.
  • Delete everything by clearing the app's storage (Android: Settings → Apps → UbiFit → Storage → Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app.

Because we do not hold copies of your data on our servers, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. You are still welcome to contact us with any privacy question or request.

Security

Your data is protected by your phone's app sandbox. We recommend using a screen lock so that others with access to your phone cannot open the app.

Children

UbiFit is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Health information

UbiFit is a general wellness app, not a medical device. Its readings are estimates and are not intended to diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. See our Terms of Use.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date above. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will also let you know in the app.
