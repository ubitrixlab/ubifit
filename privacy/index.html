Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the UbiFit mobile app ("UbiFit", "the app") handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have. UbiFit is published by Ubitrix Intl Inc ("we", "us").

  • Effective November 10, 2026
  • Published by Ubitrix Intl Inc

The short version

UbiFit reads data from your smart ring over Bluetooth and stores it in the app on your phone. There is no UbiFit account or enabled cloud-sync service, and the app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking. Information sent to your ring, photos you choose to save, and device backups are explained below.

  • No account Nothing to sign up for
  • Stays on your phone Stored in the app sandbox
  • No ads or tracking No third-party analytics
  • About 90 days Older history is cleaned up

01

Information the app handles

The app handles the following information locally. Available readings depend on your ring and the features supported by the app on your platform.

Ring health and activity data

Heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen (SpO₂), skin temperature, stress, steps, activity, distance, calories, sleep stages, workouts, and estimated blood pressure where supported.

Profile you enter

Display name and, only if you choose to add them, date of birth, sex, height, weight, daily goals, a profile photo, and your preferred units.

Ring connection details

Your ring's advertised name, Bluetooth address or platform identifier, battery level, and firmware version. The app also remembers names you give your rings, linked to their MAC address when available or their platform identifier until it is available.

02

How the information is used

  • To show your measurements, trends, sleep, and progress toward your goals.
  • To display your profile and progress against your goals. The profile details and goals you enter are saved locally in the app; the current version does not send them to your ring.
  • To find, connect to, and stay connected with your ring.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any automated decision-making.

03

Where it is stored and for how long

  • App data is stored in private storage protected by your phone's app sandbox. Photos you explicitly save to your photo library are stored separately.
  • The app keeps approximately the most recent 90 days of measurement history. Older records are removed during normal storage cleanup when the app saves data; removal does not run on a fixed daily schedule.
  • Your profile, settings, and remembered ring names are kept separately from measurement history. Switching rings keeps saved readings and ring names. Clearing the app's storage or deleting the app removes its local records, subject to any backup you later restore.
  • On Android, the app opts out of Android's automatic app-data backup. On iOS, app data may be included in device backups according to your settings. Photos saved to your library may also sync through your photo service, such as iCloud Photos, according to your settings.
04

Sharing

The current app has no enabled service that uploads your personal or health data to UbiFit servers. It uses Bluetooth to communicate with your ring and can save a photo to your library when you ask. We do not sell or rent your health data, and the app does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting software. Device backups and photo-library services are managed by your operating system and account settings.

If a future version offers optional features that need an internet connection, such as cloud backup or syncing across devices, we will update this policy first and ask for your permission in the app before enabling those uploads.

05

Permissions

Bluetooth (nearby devices)

To find, pair with, and read data from your ring.

Location

Requested only on Android 11 and earlier, where Android requires it for Bluetooth scanning. UbiFit does not read, store, or use your location.

Foreground service

Keeps the connection to your ring alive while it syncs on Android. How the service notification appears depends on your Android version and notification settings.

Camera

Used when you open Ring camera in UbiFit on supported iOS rings. The photo remains in memory until you choose Save photo.

Photos

Lets you choose a profile photo, which is resized and stored inside the app. When you choose Save photo in Ring camera on iOS, the app requests permission to add that photo to your library. Saving to Photos is separate from storing your profile image.

You can withdraw any permission in your phone's settings. Some features stop working without it.

06

Your choices

  • Edit or remove your profile details in the app at any time.
  • Use Pair a new ring on the Ring tab to choose another ring. Your current ring remains remembered until the new one connects successfully and its pairing is saved. Cancelling or a failed attempt keeps the previous pairing record. This does not guarantee an uninterrupted Bluetooth connection during an attempt.
  • Replacing a ring keeps its saved readings and your name for it, so the name can be restored when you pair it again. It does not remove the ring from your phone's Bluetooth settings.
  • Remove local app data by clearing the app's storage (Android: Settings → Apps → UbiFit → Storage → Clear storage) or deleting the app. This does not delete photos already saved to your library, device backups, or data stored on your ring; manage those separately.

Because the app does not upload its stored records to our servers, we cannot access, correct, or delete those local records on your behalf. You are still welcome to contact us with any privacy question or request.

07

Security

Your data is protected by your phone's app sandbox. We recommend using a screen lock so that others with access to your phone cannot open the app.

08

Children

UbiFit is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

09

Health information

UbiFit is a general wellness app, not a medical device. Its readings are estimates and are not intended to diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. See our Terms of Use.

10

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date above. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will also let you know in the app.
